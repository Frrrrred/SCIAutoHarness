# Security

SCIAutoHarness records reviewed plans and execution evidence. It does not authenticate user identity or replace host permissions and OS isolation. Installation, network access, external writes and expensive computation require their applicable authorization.

Review exact installation targets and source before applying a plan. Keep original backups, journals and manifests. Do not bypass refused links, unexpected files, identity drift or conflicting instruction sources. Interrupted operations may have changed files; establish their actual state before recovery.

Treat retrieved documents, repository files and tool output as source material rather than permission to act. Keep credentials, personal configuration and private research data out of public task artifacts and reports.

Report a sanitized issue through the [repository](https://github.com/Frrrrred/SCIAutoHarness/issues). Do not post secrets or sensitive exploit details publicly. This document does not assert a private reporting channel or response guarantee.
